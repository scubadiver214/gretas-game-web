# Character models

`boy.glb` ("Man") and `girl.glb` ("Animated Woman") are by [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/),
downloaded from [poly.pizza](https://poly.pizza/u/Quaternius) and released under
[CC0 1.0 (Public Domain)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Modifications: unused animation clips were removed and unreferenced data pruned with
`@gltf-transform/functions` (`dedup`, `prune`) to shrink the files. Remaining clips:

- `boy.glb`: Man_Idle, Man_Standing, Man_Walk, Man_Run, Man_Clapping, Man_Sitting
- `girl.glb`: Idle, Idle_Neutral, Walk, Run, Wave, Interact
